## Problem brief

Given positive integer weights, repeatedly return an index with probability proportional to that index's weight.

## Example

Weights `[1,3]` mean index `0` has probability `1/4` and index `1` has probability `3/4`.

## Constraints

`1 <= len(w) <= 10^4`; weights are positive; up to `10^4` picks are requested.
