# 198. House Robber

**Category:** 1-D Dynamic Programming  
**Difficulty:** Medium

[NeetCode problem](https://neetcode.io/problems/house-robber/) | [LeetCode problem](https://leetcode.com/problems/house-robber/) | [Blind 75 list](https://neetcode.io/practice/practice/blind75)

## Task

Each element gives the money in a house along a street. Return the maximum amount you can take without taking from two adjacent houses. You may choose to take from no houses.

## Example

```text
nums = [3, 2, 5, 10, 7] -> 15
```
