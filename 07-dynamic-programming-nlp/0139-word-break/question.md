## Problem brief

Determine whether a string can be segmented into a space-separated sequence of one or more dictionary words. Dictionary entries may be reused.

## Example

`s = "leetcode"`, `wordDict = ["leet", "code"]` → `True`

## Constraints

`1 <= len(s) <= 300`; dictionary words are unique lowercase strings.
