# 79. Word Search

**Category:** Backtracking  
**Difficulty:** Medium

[NeetCode problem](https://neetcode.io/problems/search-for-word/) | [LeetCode problem](https://leetcode.com/problems/word-search/) | [Blind 75 list](https://neetcode.io/practice/practice/blind75)

## Task

Determine whether a word can be formed by a path through a rectangular character grid. Consecutive letters must use horizontally or vertically adjacent cells, and a cell may be used at most once in a path.

## Example

```text
board = [["A", "B"], ["C", "D"]], word = "ABD" -> true
```
