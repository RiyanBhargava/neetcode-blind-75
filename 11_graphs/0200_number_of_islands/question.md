# 200. Number of Islands

**Category:** Graphs  
**Difficulty:** Medium

[NeetCode problem](https://neetcode.io/problems/count-number-of-islands/) | [LeetCode problem](https://leetcode.com/problems/number-of-islands/) | [Blind 75 list](https://neetcode.io/practice/practice/blind75)

## Task

Count the islands in a rectangular grid of characters 1 and 0. An island is a maximal group of land cells connected horizontally or vertically; diagonal contact does not connect islands. The area outside the grid is water.

## Example

```text
grid = [["1", "0"], ["0", "1"]] -> 2
```
