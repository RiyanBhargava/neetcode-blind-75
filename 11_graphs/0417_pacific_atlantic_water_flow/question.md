# 417. Pacific Atlantic Water Flow

**Category:** Graphs  
**Difficulty:** Medium

[NeetCode problem](https://neetcode.io/problems/pacific-atlantic-water-flow/) | [LeetCode problem](https://leetcode.com/problems/pacific-atlantic-water-flow/) | [Blind 75 list](https://neetcode.io/practice/practice/blind75)

## Task

A height grid touches the Pacific on its top and left borders and the Atlantic on its bottom and right borders. Water can flow horizontally or vertically to a cell of equal or lower height. Return all coordinates from which water can reach both oceans.

## Example

```text
heights = [[1, 2], [4, 3]] -> [[0, 1], [1, 0], [1, 1]]
```
