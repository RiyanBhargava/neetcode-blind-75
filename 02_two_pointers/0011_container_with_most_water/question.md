# 11. Container With Most Water

**Category:** Two Pointers  
**Difficulty:** Medium

[NeetCode problem](https://neetcode.io/problems/max-water-container/) | [LeetCode problem](https://leetcode.com/problems/container-with-most-water/) | [Blind 75 list](https://neetcode.io/practice/practice/blind75)

## Task

Each array element gives the height of a vertical line at its index. Choose two lines that, together with the horizontal axis, hold the most water. Return that area; the container cannot be tilted.

## Example

```text
height = [2, 5, 4, 3] -> 6
```
